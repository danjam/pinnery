Pinnery

Connect to your server

Connect opens your Pinnery server, where you sign in (if you aren't already) and approve this browser. The extension gets its own token — it never sees your password. Tokens are listed and revoked from Pinnery's settings page.

Connected

To use the extension:

  1. Right-click any image on a web page.
  2. Choose Pin to Pinnery.
  3. Pick a board and click Pin.
